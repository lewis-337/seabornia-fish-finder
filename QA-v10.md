Screenshots and machine-readable browser receipts are kept in the vault QA folder `Files/Company/IT/Seabornia Fish Finder/QA/2026-10-24-fishing-maps/`. The source repository retains the audits and reproducible check scripts; private user records were not accessed or uploaded.


The first live collection added newly recognized source fish names and revealed 10 unique split/combined-name duplicates across two free-text adapters. Original combined rows and quantities are retained; redundant unquantified component rows are suppressed. Distinct quantified rows and different outings are preserved. A real scheduled-refresh regression test covers repeated imports, not only the pure helper. Raw aliases are retained as fish_reported while verified names become canonical. No client asset changes in this data-only correction.

The live refresh also surfaced195 Katakura records from before the24-month window. Those valid source facts are preserved in data/archive/katakura-outside-window-v10.json; they are excluded from active packs and recent species/season evidence. The collector now enforces a calendar24-month lower bound as well as the Japanese current-day upper bound. A deterministic-clock regression test covers old archive rows and leap-day cutoffs. Extraction placeholders for free-text articles say 未確認 rather than claiming the original article omitted the information.

Push releases now publish the locally reviewed public datasets directly. Six-hour catch collection and hourly conditions collection continue on their existing schedules; manual collection remains available. This avoids external data changing midway through a reviewed source-code release. Unfilled free-text fields are labelled unconfirmed; generic notes do not claim the source omitted data that the adapter has not extracted.
