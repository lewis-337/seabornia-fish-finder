# Photo audit v10

141/141 catalogue entries decode; 136 unique JPEG assets. All 84 additions were visually reviewed on contact sheets after replacing unrelated objects, illustrations, maps and a document thumbnail. Existing 57 entries retain their previous review.

No synthetic fish imagery is used. Group/growth-stage photos have representative/example labels. ギス uses the real frame grab identified as Pterothrissus gissu in Aguzzi et al. 2018, Figure 2K (CC BY 4.0), cropped with the change recorded in credits. ヒレコダイ is a photographed museum specimen. スギ is an identified juvenile reference.

Three reviewed exceptions use CC BY-NC 4.0: ソコイトヨリ (FishBase, D.G.R. Wiadnya), アコウダイ and ニベ (individual iNaturalist observations). This release retains the approved personal/non-commercial scope. These entries explicitly carry noncommercial_only metadata and the UI displays 非商用利用限定. They must be replaced or separately licensed before commercial use. Other photographs use CC0, public domain, CC BY or CC BY-SA. Unreviewed NC images remain rejected by the automatic Commons path.
